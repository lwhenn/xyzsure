How AI Compliance Tool Works

Small clinical laboratories are held to the same CLIA expectations as much larger ones, with far less staff to write procedures, track competency, and get ready for survey. XYZSure was built inside XYZ Laboratory for that gap. It helps your team organize CLIA documentation, spot what is missing, and keep the file current between inspections. The laboratory director stays responsible. XYZSure makes the work faster and more consistent.

XYZSure works from your compliance checklist, your procedures and records, and live LIMS data — so a small team can see what is in place, what is missing, and what to fix before inspection.

  1. 1.Gap analysis

    Select requirements and let AI flag what is in place, what is missing, and the corrective action for each.

  2. 2.Evidence search

    Pull survey-ready summaries and ranked excerpts from your procedures, validations, and records for each requirement.

  3. 3.Remediation

    Track fixes, follow up with questions, and export results to Google Sheets to keep the file current between inspections.
