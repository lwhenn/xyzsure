Welcome to AI Compliance

AI Compliance validates CAP checklist requirements using live LIMS data, indexed documents, and AI analysis.


AI Compliance supports every critical function, including:

  • AI Gap Analysis: Workbook-style assessment against CAP requirements, with evidence search and remediation tracking.
  • Evidence Search: Audit-ready AI summaries and ranked document excerpts for each selected requirement.
  • Remediation Tracking: Corrective actions and completion notes that can update requirement status when criteria are met.
  • Search Results & Viewer: Browse saved compliance reports and inspect a single result in detail.
  • Document List: Review indexed SOPs and validation documents used as AI compliance evidence.
